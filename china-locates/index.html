What changedEngineering that earns trust

The platform is designed around operational reality: the inventory must be current, readers must agree, and a degraded dependency must fail safely.

01

Fast by construction

Targeted Redis queries replaced full-table scans, taking reconciliation and short-sell endpoints from roughly 30 seconds to sub-second.

02

Consistent across pods

A leader-follower topology with heartbeat-gated reads eliminates cross-pod divergence while keeping readers horizontally scalable.

03

Observable in production

Prometheus coverage grew from health checks to around 15 operational metrics for SLO tracking and quicker incident triage.
